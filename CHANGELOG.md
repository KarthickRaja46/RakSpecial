# What's new in RAK Special

Each section below becomes the release notes on GitHub and in the app's 'Update available' window.
Write for the people who use the app: short, plain lines, one change per line.

## 2.1.7
- New 'Last cheque printed' card under the preview: number, amount, payee, date and status of your last cheque.
- Void a cheque in one click: 'Void' on the last cheque, or 'Void cheque' under the form for any cheque number, including a spoilt blank leaf.
- After voiding, RAK Special offers to print the same cheque again on the next leaf.
- The figures under the preview are bigger and easier to read, and the cheque picture is sharper.
- On smaller laptop screens the window now fits the screen instead of running off the bottom.

## 2.1.6
- The 'Update available' window now always shows what's new in the update.
- Release notes for every version are kept in one place, so each update explains itself.

## 2.1.5
- Payees now have a category (Rent, Supplier, Salary, Utility, Government, Loan, Other, or your own), phone and email.
- Analytics: new 'By category' tab with a chart and totals per category, and a Category filter.
- Analytics: new 'Cheques' tab. Remove test prints or mistakes from analytics, and put them back at any time. They stay in the cheque register.
- 'Set category...' on Analytics > By payee, to set a payee's category in one click.

## 2.1.4
- Cheques now print correctly: the cheque goes into the printer standing up, centred in the tray.
- New Print window with an 'Insert the cheque' picture that shows exactly how to put the cheque in.
- The Windows print window is no longer used, so your page settings always stay correct.

## 2.1.3
- Printing goes to your real printer; PDF "printers" are no longer chosen by mistake.

## 2.1.2
- Updates install from inside the app: an 'Update available' window opens when a new version is out. 'Update now' downloads, installs and reopens RAK Special.

## 2.1.1
- The RAK Business and RAK Old cheque pictures are built in, so the preview shows the real cheque straight after installing.

## 2.1.0
- New modern design: cheque form, live preview and this month's figures on one screen.
- Analytics: totals by month and payee, cheques awaiting clearing, bounced cheques and upcoming post-dated cheques.
- Cheque status: Printed, Cleared, Bounced, Stopped or Void.
- Help & support window with email and WhatsApp.
- Setup wizard and automatic update check.
