RAK Special is installed. Before your first real cheque, do these steps once:

1. CHOOSE YOUR CHEQUE BOOK
   At the top of the window, pick the book in your hand:
     RAK Business (RAK-001)  - new business cheque, 202 x 99 mm
     RAK Old      (RAK-002)  - older, wider book,   206 x 100 mm
   The amount box is in a different place on each book, so the
   wrong choice prints the figures in the wrong place.

2. TEST ON PLAIN PAPER
   Click "Test print" (on plain paper). Hold the sheet against a real
   cheque in front of a light and check that the text lines up.

3. ADJUST IF NEEDED
   Everything shifted the same way:
     Tools > Layout editor > Cheque & printer settings > Offset X / Offset Y
   Only one field is off:
     Tools > Layout editor, then drag that field.

4. PRINT
   Press Print cheque (Ctrl+P). Put the cheque in the printer tray
   standing up, narrow side first, centred between the paper guides.
   The Print window shows a picture of exactly how.

Tips
  - Tools > PDC series prints rent or instalment cheques in one go.
  - File > Backup data now and then.
  - Analytics (Ctrl+T) shows where the money went, by month, payee
    and category, and the post-dated cheques still to come.
  - Made a mistake? The "Last cheque printed" card has Void and Edit.
  - Your data is kept only on this computer, in %APPDATA%\RAK Special.
    No account, no cloud. Uninstalling or updating does not delete it.
  - RAK Special is free and open source (MIT License).
