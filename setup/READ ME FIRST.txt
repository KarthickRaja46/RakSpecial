RAK SPECIAL - RAK Bank cheque printing
=======================================

WHAT IT DOES
  - Prints RAK Bank cheques on an ordinary printer (A4 landscape,
    cheque placed in the top-left corner against the paper guide).
  - Two RAK cheque books - pick the one in your hand:
      RAK Business  (RAK-001)  new business cheque, 202 x 99 mm
      RAK Old       (RAK-002)  older, wider book,    206 x 100 mm
    On the Old book the amount box sits lower than on the Business
    book, so the wrong choice prints the figures in the wrong place.
  - Amount in words with fils (e.g. 1250.50), payee list, cheque
    register, duplicate-number warning, confirm box before printing.
  - Preview shows your cheque on a picture of the real RAK cheque.
  - Tools > PDC series: rent / instalment cheques - enter the series
    once (e.g. 12 cheques, every month) and print them one by one.
  - Tools > Layout editor: move any field, change size / bold,
    printer offset for your printer.

FILES
  RAKSpecial.exe      - the program
  INSTALL.bat         - installs it (no administrator needed)
  UNINSTALL.bat       - removes it (your data is kept)
  READ ME FIRST.txt   - this file

HOW TO INSTALL
  1. Right-click the ZIP -> "Extract All..." -> Extract.
  2. Open the extracted folder and double-click INSTALL.bat.
     If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click
     "More info" -> "Run anyway".
  3. Wait for "[DONE]" and press any key.
  4. Open "RAK Special" from the Desktop.
  To update later: run the new INSTALL.bat the same way.
  Your data stays in %APPDATA%\RAK Special.

BEFORE THE FIRST REAL CHEQUE
  1. Choose the cheque book (RAK Business or RAK Old).
  2. Click "Test print on plain paper". It prints the cheque picture
     with your text on top. Hold it against a real cheque in front
     of a light.
  3. If everything is shifted the same way: Tools > Layout editor >
     [ Cheque & printer settings ] > Offset X / Offset Y (mm).
     If one field is off, drag it in the layout editor.
  4. Use File > Backup data now and then.
